# CUSTOMER-RETENTION-AND-CHURN-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS


## Interoduction

Customer retention is an important factor in the growth and profitability of any subscription-based business. Understanding customer behaviour, subscription patterns, service preferences, and payment methods helps organizations identify potential retention challenges and make informed business decisions.

This project focuses on analysing customer subscription data to understand customer distribution, service usage, payment preferences, and revenue patterns. The analysis was carried out using **Microsoft Power BI** to transform raw customer data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and dashboard reporting.

The dashboard provides an overview of customer subscriptions, total charges, monthly revenue, and different service categories, allowing stakeholders to explore customer patterns and identify areas that may require improvement.

##  Project Objectives

The major objectives of this analysis were to:

- Analyse the overall customer subscription base.
- Examine total charges and monthly revenue generated from customers.
- Understand customer distribution across different tenure periods.
- Evaluate the relationship between internet service types and total charges.
- Analyse customer preferences for available payment methods.
- Examine customer usage of online security and technical support services.
- Understand subscription patterns across different contract types.
- Provide actionable recommendations that can improve customer retention and business performance.

##  Dataset Description

The analysis was conducted using a customer subscription and churn dataset containing **7,043 customer records**.

The dataset contains information relating to customer subscriptions, service usage, payment preferences, tenure, and financial charges.

Key areas explored include:

| Data Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer Information | Customer subscription records |
| Tenure | Length of time customers have subscribed |
| Internet Services | Fiber optic, DSL, and no internet service |
| Phone Service | Availability and usage of phone services |
| Online Security | Customer subscription to online security |
| Technical Support | Customer access to technical support |
| Payment Method | Electronic check, mailed check, bank transfer, and credit card |
| Contract Type | Month-to-month, one-year, and two-year contracts |
| Financial Information | Total charges and monthly charges |
| Churn | Customer subscription cancellation status |

##  Tools Used
The following tools were used in the project:

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development, data visualization, and interactive reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Creation of calculated measures and analytical metrics.
- **Data Visualization:** Charts, cards, slicers, and other visual elements used to communicate findings.

## Data Analysis Process

The analysis followed these major stages:

### Step 1: Data Collection
The customer subscription dataset was obtained and examined to understand its structure, variables, and analytical relevance.

### Step 2: Data Cleaning and Preparation
The dataset was reviewed and prepared for analysis. This stage focused on checking data consistency, ensuring appropriate data types, and preparing the relevant fields for visualization.

### Step 3: Data Modelling and DAX
Relevant measures and calculations were developed to support the analysis of customer counts, total charges, and monthly charges.

### Step 4: Data Visualization
Different visualizations were selected to communicate the findings clearly. These included bar charts, column charts, pie charts, doughnut charts, and KPI cards.

### Step 5: Dashboard Development
An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed with slicers that allow users to explore customer information based on churn, phone service, online security, streaming TV, streaming movies, and technical support.

##  Dashboard Features and Analysis

The dashboard contains three major KPI cards and eight visualizations.

### A. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Subscribers: 7,043** – Represents the total number of customer records analysed.
- **Total Charges: $16.1 Million** – Shows the cumulative charges recorded across customers.
- **Total Monthly Charges: $456.1K** – Represents the combined monthly charges.

### B. Dashboard Visualizations

**1. Total Charges by Internet Service**

This visualization compares the total charges generated across different internet service categories.

- Fiber Optic: $9.9M
- DSL: $5.1M
- No Internet Service: $1.0M

The analysis shows that Fiber Optic customers contribute the largest share of total charges, followed by DSL customers.

**2. Subscribers by Tenure Year Trend**

This chart examines the distribution of subscribers across different tenure periods.

It provides insight into how customers are distributed according to the length of their subscriptions and helps identify the concentration of customers within different tenure categories.

**3. Total Charges Trend by Contract Type**

This visualization compares total charges generated by customers across three contract categories:

- Two-Year Contract: $6M
- Month-to-Month Contract: $5M
- One-Year Contract: $4M

The two-year contract category records the highest total charges, while the one-year contract category records the lowest among the three categories.

**4. Total Subscribers to Online Security**

This chart examines customer subscription to online security services.

- No: 3.5K (49.67%)
- Yes: 2.02K (28.67%)
- No Internet Service: 1.53K (21.67%)

The result indicates that customers without online security represent the largest category, highlighting an opportunity for improved awareness and promotion of additional security services.

**5. Total Phone Service Subscribers**

This visualization shows the distribution of customers based on phone service subscriptions.

- Yes: 6,361 (90.32%)
- No: 682 (9.68%)

The majority of customers subscribe to phone services, indicating strong adoption of this service.

**6. Preferred Payment Method by Subscribers**

This chart examines the payment methods used by customers.

- Electronic Check: 2,365 (33.58%)
- Mailed Check: 1,612 (22.89%)
- Bank Transfer: 1,544 (21.92%)
- Credit Card: 1,522 (21.61%)

Electronic check is the most commonly used payment method, while credit card records the lowest number of subscribers among the four payment categories.

**7. Subscribers' Rate of Subscribing to Technical Support**

This visualization examines customer subscription to technical support services.

- No: 3.5K
- Yes: 2.0K
- No Internet Service: 1.5K

The result indicates that a larger number of customers do not subscribe to technical support compared to those who do.

**8. Monthly Charges by Multiple Lines Subscribers**

This chart compares monthly charges across multiple-line subscription categories.

- Yes: $244K
- No: $184K
- No Phone Service: $29K

Customers with multiple lines generate the highest combined monthly charges among the categories displayed.

## 7. Key Insights and Findings

The analysis revealed the following important findings:

1. **Customer Revenue Contribution:** Fiber Optic customers account for the highest total charges, making this service category an important contributor to overall revenue.

2. **Contract Performance:** Two-year contracts generate the highest total charges among the contract categories analysed.

3. **Service Adoption:** Phone service has a high adoption rate, with approximately 90.32% of customers subscribing to the service.

4. **Payment Behaviour:** Electronic checks are the most preferred payment method, accounting for approximately 33.58% of customers.

5. **Additional Service Opportunities:** A considerable proportion of customers do not subscribe to online security and technical support, presenting opportunities for service promotion.

6. **Multiple-Line Revenue:** Customers with multiple lines contribute the highest combined monthly charges among the multiple-line categories.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

- **Improve Customer Retention:** The organization should investigate customer churn patterns and introduce targeted retention strategies, especially for customers who may be at risk of cancelling their subscriptions.

- **Promote Long-Term Contracts:** Since two-year contracts generate the highest total charges, the business can encourage longer subscriptions through attractive discounts and loyalty benefits.

- **Expand Additional Services:** The organization should create awareness campaigns and attractive subscription packages for online security and technical support.

- **Strengthen Revenue Opportunities:** Since Fiber Optic customers contribute the highest total charges, the organization should continue improving service quality and customer satisfaction within this category.

- **Improve Payment Experience:** The organization should evaluate payment preferences and provide convenient payment options to improve customer experience.

- **Encourage Multiple-Line Subscriptions:** The business can develop bundled packages and promotional offers to attract more customers to multiple-line services.


##  Conclusion

The Customer Retention and Churn Performance Analysis demonstrates the importance of using data to understand customer behaviour and subscription performance.

Through interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring, the dashboard provides an overview of customer distribution, service adoption, payment preferences, and revenue contribution.

The insights generated can help organizations identify opportunities to improve service adoption, strengthen customer relationships, and make more informed business decisions.
